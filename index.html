• Relational aspects of design englobe features of the artefact that will be perceived by someone else and work as vectors for an idea.
  • Technical aspects of design relate to infrastructure allowing for the artefact to perform its function.
  • All design tasks are situated somewhere on the technical-relational spectrum. Every designer will place things differently.
  • The relational aspect allows us to build culture together, as producers we enable the viewers. This aspect of the work should remain a collaboration between humans. It relies on our sensibility rather than on logic. Commonplace aesthetics and concepts do not help push culture forward.

  • We should be proficient in all aspects of technicality that are part of the cultural discourse. However, to bridge gaps in our skillset and allow us to transmit the original idea in the best way we imagined, AI can become a valuable tool.
  • Exception always for any project having AI at the heart of its cultural discourse.
